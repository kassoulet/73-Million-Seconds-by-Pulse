The 3D scenes of the demo

  • aziz1.lwe / aziz2.lwe (0 s - 40 s, speed 32, start frame 200): one object (aziz_2.lwv, 789 vertices, 1187 triangles: a sphere with bubbles) that never moves, a white light that flies around it on a 12-key spline and a camera on 10 keys that dives through the sphere and orbits it (frames 200-1500). aziz1 is rendered into SCREEN16 as shadow-volume beams, aziz2 draws the environment-mapped translucent surface (lwotr 57) into SCREEN; the 2D effects add the blur, palette and fades.
  • vol.lwe (48 s - 79 s, 4 fps): p_u.lwv, 68 vertices / 34 triangles (echo2.png), 17 position keys, static camera, 3-key light; filtered and radially blurred by 2D effects.
  • smoke.exp (88 s - 106 s, 69 fps): a GeoSphere (162 vertices) that rises along +y, a camera flying through a tunnel on six keys (position and target tracks) and five lights f2.source ... f2.source04 that are not drawn as geometry: setupflares / flareparticles read their animated positions through the scene API.
  • box3.exp (106 s - 121 s, 50 fps) and box2.exp (234 s -, 40 fps): two boxes, Box01 (echo.png / pink, opacity 160) and Box02 (envb.jpg); their geometry is replaced every frame by the marching cubes (marchingcubes ... jabole box02 OurVolume), and for kubes renderoff disables the polygon drawing while volumesprites draws sprites at the volume points through the scene camera.
  • obj1.lwe (188 s - 211 s, 3 fps): obj11.lwv (3352 vertices, 6696 triangles) with a rotation of 720° per 160 frames, a null on a 9-key path, a static light and a 4-key camera; displace247 sdfdfg {skeen obj11.lwv 0.15 0.015 1.27} makes the vertices pulse radially: v' = c + (v - c)(1 + 0.15 sin(|v-c|*1.27 + 0.015*Frame)) around the centroid.
  • scene.lwe (Particl3, 11 keys, with a type-11 modifier chunk) is loaded by the script but never played.
Not used by the script
bomb, displacenorm, setwireframe, setparticles
